1. Core Trigonometric Derivatives Applied
Sine Derivative:
d/dt [sin(t)] = cos(t)
Chain: d/dt [A sin(Bt)] = AB cos(Bt)
Cosine Derivative:
d/dt [cos(t)] = -sin(t)
Chain: d/dt [C cos(Dt)] = -CD sin(Dt)
Tangent Derivative:
d/dt [tan(t)] = sec²(t)
Chain: d/dt [A tan(Bt)] = AB sec²(Bt)
2. Tangent Line Launch Ramp & Chain Rule Training

The launch ramp is modeled by the sinusoidal profile y(x) = 2 sin(x). The instantaneous tangent slope is obtained directly by differentiation: m = y'(x₀) = 2 cos(x₀). The physical launch angle is θ = arctan(m), directly controlling 3D projectile ballistic trajectory!

In the Pull-Up Workout station, vertical body displacement is h(t) = sin(ωt). By the Chain Rule, vertical velocity is h'(t) = ω cos(ωt). Timing rep clicks near peak velocity grants a 2x XP bonus!
